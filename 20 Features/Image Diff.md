# Image and Binary Diff

**Group:** Diff, compare and merge  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 4 of 66

## What it does
Visual comparison of images (swipe, fade, side by side) and sometimes binary or hex views.

## Why it matters
Design and game repos need more than 'binary files differ'.

## Git equivalent
`git diff` shows nothing useful; external diff drivers needed

## What to check when evaluating
Which formats? Are onion-skin or swipe modes available?
