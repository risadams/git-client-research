# Multi-Repository Management

**Group:** Hosting and workflow integrations  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 4 of 66

## What it does
Open, group, and act on many repositories at once (tabs, workspaces, dashboards).

## Why it matters
Helpful for microservices and multi-package projects.

## Git equivalent
Scripts looping over directories, `git submodule foreach`

## What to check when evaluating
Can you run fetch/pull across a group? Is status shown in a single dashboard?
