# Hosting Service Integrations

**Group:** Hosting and workflow integrations  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 15 of 66

## What it does
Sign in to GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Azure DevOps, or Gitea to clone, list repos, and see status.

## Why it matters
Skips credential and remote setup.

## Git equivalent
`git clone <url>`, credential helpers

## What to check when evaluating
Self-hosted and enterprise variants supported? OAuth or token?
