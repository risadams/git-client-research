# Merge Conflict Resolver (3-way)

**Group:** Diff, compare and merge  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 16 of 66

## What it does
A dedicated view showing your side, their side, and the result, with controls to pick or edit lines.

## Why it matters
Conflicts are where most people lose time or break things.

## Git equivalent
`git mergetool`, editing conflict markers by hand

## What to check when evaluating
Is the output pane editable? Does it support ours/theirs previews and word-level conflicts? Does it show the base?
