# Blame

**Group:** History and investigation  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 10 of 66

## What it does
Shows which commit and author last changed each line of a file.

## Why it matters
Fastest way to find out why a line looks the way it does.

## Git equivalent
`git blame`

## What to check when evaluating
Can you click through to the parent commit and follow renames and moved code?
