# AI Commit Messages

**Group:** AI assistance  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 5 of 66

## What it does
Generates a commit message from the staged diff.

## Why it matters
Saves time but requires reviewing output; quality varies.

## Git equivalent
n/a

## What to check when evaluating
Which model or provider? Is your diff sent to a third party? Can you bring your own key or use a local model?
