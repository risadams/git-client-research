# Drag and Drop Operations

**Group:** Branching models and history rewriting  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 3 of 66

## What it does
Perform merges, rebases, cherry-picks, or reorders by dragging branches or commits onto each other.

## Why it matters
Discoverability for people who think visually.

## Git equivalent
`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`

## What to check when evaluating
Does it confirm the action before running? Are the resulting commands shown?
