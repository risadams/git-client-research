# Diff Viewer

**Group:** Diff, compare and merge  
**Clients with this feature in my notes:** 17 of 66

## What it does
Shows what changed in a file, either inline or side by side.

## Why it matters
The core of reviewing your own work before committing.

## Git equivalent
`git diff`

## What to check when evaluating
Are word-level changes highlighted? Can you ignore whitespace? Is large-file performance acceptable?
