## Observations

- The paid cluster sits between roughly $48 and $149 per user per year. The one-time licenses (Fork, Sublime Merge) become cheaper than subscriptions after one to three years.
- At 25 users the range is about $1,200 to $3,725 per year for subscriptions. A free client costs $0 in licenses but moves cost to training and tooling gaps.
- Tower is the highest-priced mainstream option and the only one verified with three tiers on its pricing page.
- GitKraken Pro: third-party pages show about $4 (Capterra, unit unclear) to $5 per user per month, which is $48 to $60 per year and matches the $48 floor. Advanced, Business and Enterprise cloud prices were still not retrieved.
- Sublime Merge price evidence conflicts: $99 (Slashdot), $80 (upgrade page), $152 (bundle with Sublime Text), and a business subscription starting at $65 per seat per year (Tekpon, which describes Sublime Text). Verify on the store page before budgeting.

## Pricing models in the market

| Model | Vendors | Buyer implication |
|---|---|---|
| Free platform funnel | Microsoft, Atlassian | No budget approval; cost is lock-in |
| Freemium tiers | GitKraken, GitButler | Free to start; paywall on private repos, teams, or AI |
| Annual subscription | Tower, SmartGit | Predictable support; recurring cost |
| One-time license | Fork, Sublime Merge | Cheapest over 3+ years; upgrades may cost |
| Education and non-profit free | Tower, SmartGit | Strong student funnel |
