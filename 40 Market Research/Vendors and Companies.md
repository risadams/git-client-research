## Vendor profiles

### GitHub (Microsoft)

- Product: [[GitHub Desktop|GitHub Desktop]]
- Ownership: Microsoft subsidiary
- HQ: San Francisco, US
- Founded: GitHub 2007; Desktop open-sourced
- Team: Large (Microsoft)
- Funding: None needed: free funnel into GitHub
- Revenue model: Free; drives GitHub platform adoption
- User claims: none stated
- Sources: github.com/desktop/desktop

### Atlassian

- Product: [[Sourcetree|Sourcetree]]
- Ownership: Atlassian Corporation Plc (public)
- HQ: Sydney, AU / San Francisco, US
- Founded: Atlassian 2002
- Team: Large (public company)
- Funding: Public; Atlassian ~$6.2B trailing-twelve-month revenue per getpanto (secondary)
- Revenue model: Free; funnel into Bitbucket and Jira
- User claims: none stated
- Sources: getpanto.ai Bitbucket statistics; PeerSpot; TrustRadius

### GitKraken (formerly Axosoft)

- Product: [[GitKraken Desktop|GitKraken Desktop]]
- Ownership: Private; equity investment from Resurgens Technology Partners (2020-09-03)
- HQ: Scottsdale, AZ, US; office in Alicante, ES
- Founded: 2002 per Wikipedia (another source says 2000)
- Team: 40 to 90+ staff by 2021 (yespress, low reliability)
- Funding: Private equity growth capital; amount undisclosed
- Revenue model: Freemium plus per-seat tiers; DevEx suite (GitLens, Jira integration)
- User claims: '2M users since 2016' (2020 CB Insights quote); 6M combined Git users after BigBrassBand deal; marketing claims 40M+ developers (vendor claim)
- Sources: Wikipedia; Resurgens press; CB Insights; privsource; GitKraken help center

### fournova Software GmbH

- Product: [[Tower|Tower]]
- Ownership: Private; founders; smartgit.dev lists it as part of SaaS.group
- HQ: Stuttgart, DE (one directory says Saarbruecken)
- Founded: 2010 (Tobias Guenther, Julian Rothkamp)
- Team: 10 to 50 (startups.fm)
- Funding: Bootstrapped per startups.fm (secondary)
- Revenue model: Annual subscription (Basic, Pro, Enterprise); free for students and educators
- User claims: '100,000+ users' (vendor claim); 50,000 in a 2015 press release
- Sources: git-tower.com docs; Perforce press release 2015; startups.fm; marketscreener

### syntevo GmbH

- Product: [[SmartGit|SmartGit]]
- Ownership: Private; vendor says owned by European individuals
- HQ: Ainring, DE (imprint)
- Founded: Syntevo 2001; SmartGit 2009
- Team: 11 to 50 (LinkedIn); 16 to 50 (WeAreDevelopers)
- Funding: Self-funded (no funding found)
- Revenue model: Free for non-commercial; commercial subscription; free for academics, open source, charities since 2025
- User claims: 'thousands of developers' (vendor interview)
- Sources: smartgit.dev imprint and vendor page; syntevo 20-year interview

### Sublime HQ Pty Ltd

- Product: [[Sublime Merge|Sublime Merge]]
- Ownership: Private, small
- HQ: Sydney, AU
- Founded: Sublime Text 2008; Merge released 2018-09-20 (Jon Skinner)
- Team: 1 to 10 (startups.fm); forum names about 6 staff
- Funding: Bootstrapped (forum and startups.fm, secondary)
- Revenue model: One-time license with paid upgrades; business subscription; bundle with Sublime Text
- User claims: none stated
- Sources: sublimehq.com store; Sublime forum; startups.fm; SourceForge

### GitButler Inc.

- Product: [[GitButler|GitButler]]
- Ownership: VC-backed startup
- HQ: Berlin, DE (also US)
- Founded: 2023 (Scott Chacon CEO, Kiril Videlov CTO, Anne Leuschner)
- Team: 11 to 50 (ai-market-watch, secondary)
- Funding: Series A $17M, 2026-04-08, led by a16z (Peter Levine joins board), with Fly Ventures and A.Capital; seed about $3.7M in 2023 (secondary)
- Revenue model: Free for individuals today; fair-source license; paid team features implied by the funding story, not verified
- User claims: none stated
- Sources: blog.gitbutler.com/series-a; SiliconANGLE; techstackups

## Business model patterns

| Model | Vendors | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free funnel | Microsoft, Atlassian | Unlimited runway | Feature priority follows the platform, not the client; Sourcetree updates lag |
| PE-backed suite | GitKraken | Acquisition budget | Pricing pressure to expand seats |
| Bootstrapped indie | fournova, syntevo, Sublime HQ, Fork | Stable, profitable focus | Small teams; key-person risk |
| VC-backed startup | GitButler | Fast iteration | Must find revenue; license is fair-source, not fully open |
