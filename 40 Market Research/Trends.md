# Trends

| # | Trend | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI coding agents create demand for parallel branches and change review | GitButler Series A ($17M, a16z, Apr 2026); Entire $60M seed (Feb 2026); Stack Overflow 2025: 84% use or plan to use AI tools, Claude Code 10% and Cursor 18% usage among respondents | Strong funding signal, weak usage proof |
| 2 | Developer trust in AI output is falling while usage rises | Stack Overflow 2025: 46% distrust accuracy, up from 31% the year before | Survey-based |
| 3 | Stacked branches and stacked pull requests go mainstream | Tower lists stacked branches and native Graphite; GitButler stacked branches | Moderate |
| 4 | Undo and safety nets as selling points | Tower's "Undo Anything" is its headline differentiator | Moderate |
| 5 | Consolidation and bundling | GitKraken acquired BigBrassBand, GitLens, CodeSee; PE ownership since 2020 | Strong |
| 6 | Free tiers widen | SmartGit free for academics, open source, charities (2025); Tower free for students | Moderate |
| 7 | Open-source terminal and Rust/Go UIs gain mindshare | LazyGit 81.6k stars, GitUI 22.6k stars | Strong interest, unknown usage |
| 8 | Editors absorb Git | VS Code and Visual Studio top IDEs in Stack Overflow 2025; GitLens, JetBrains Git | Moderate |
| 9 | Source-available licensing | GitButler uses Fair Source (FSL, converts to MIT after 2 years); Git Revenge is Apache-2.0 | Weak, small sample |
| 10 | Legacy client stagnation | Sourcetree mindshare 6.9% to 6.3% in a year (PeerSpot, category-wide) | Weak |
| 11 | New small clients appear steadily | Round 2 additions in this vault: GitComet, rgitui, ForkPlus, Gitember, BeeBranch, Stage, Gitu | Observed, anecdotal |
| 12 | AI editors buy review and stacking tools | Cursor signed a deal to acquire Graphite on 2025-12-19; price undisclosed | Strong, single deal |
| 13 | Interfaces appear around post-Git systems | Jujutsu has 32.3k stars and at least 8 community interfaces; Sapling ships a web UI. See [[Adjacent Tools]] | Moderate |

## Implications

- **Vendors without AI or stacked-branch stories** compete on polish and price only.
- **Buyers** can pilot GitButler or Tower for stacked workflows while keeping a free client as fallback.
- **Risk to watch:** a VC-backed vendor may change pricing or license terms; PE-owned vendors may raise prices.
