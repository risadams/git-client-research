# Market Segments

Segment fit follows the ratings in [[Audience Guide]]; those ratings are my judgment, not tested.

| Segment | Need | Buying behavior | Leading options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learners and students | Low friction, free | Free only | [[GitHub Desktop\|GitHub Desktop]], [[Sourcetree\|Sourcetree]], [[Tower\|Tower]] (free for students) |
| Solo professional | Speed, safety nets, one-time cost | Pays $60 to $130 | [[Fork\|Fork]], [[Sublime Merge\|Sublime Merge]], [[Tower\|Tower]] |
| Small team (2 to 10) | Consistency, shared workflow | Mixed: standardize on free or buy seats | [[GitKraken Desktop\|GitKraken Desktop]], [[SmartGit\|SmartGit]], [[Tower\|Tower]] |
| Enterprise (25+) | SSO, compliance, support, procurement | Contracts and security review | [[GitKraken Desktop\|GitKraken Desktop]] Enterprise, [[Tower\|Tower]] Enterprise, [[SmartGit\|SmartGit]] |
| Atlassian shops | Bitbucket and Jira alignment | Bundled | [[Sourcetree\|Sourcetree]] |
| Terminal-first power users | Keyboard speed, scripting | Free | [[LazyGit\|LazyGit]], [[GitUI\|GitUI]], Magit |
| Editor-centric developers | Stay in the IDE | Bundled | VS Code, JetBrains, [[GitLens (VS Code)\|GitLens]] |
| AI-agent workflows | Parallel branches, review of agent output | Early adopters | [[GitButler\|GitButler]] |
| Windows Explorer users | Shell integration | Free | [[TortoiseGit\|TortoiseGit]] |

## Segment sizing

No reliable counts exist. Proxies:

- Stack Overflow 2025 sampled 49,000+ developers across 177 countries; the sample covers developers broadly, not Git GUI users.
- Vendor claims put Tower at 100,000+ users and GitKraken at millions of sign-ups over its life.
- Bitbucket reported 15 million developers in 2025, which bounds the Sourcetree audience from above but is not an estimate of it.

A defensible estimate would need install telemetry. See [[Open Questions]].
