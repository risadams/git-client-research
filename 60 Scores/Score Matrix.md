# Score Matrix

Pilot scoring of the 27 clients that have audience ratings, generated by rule from the vault ([[Scoring Criteria]]). Results by persona are in [[Persona Results]]. Rubric: [[Scoring Rubric]].

**Cell notation.** A digit followed by an evidence grade, such as `2V` (score 2, verified client note). `0u` is unknown and scores 0. `p` means the score depends on the persona (cost and platform, shown below).

**Reading it.** A 2 means the relevant features are documented, not that they were tested. Clients with grade L, B, or S notes are floors.
